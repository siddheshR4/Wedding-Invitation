Guest Invitation Links

Type the guest names, get one personal link for each, and send it on WhatsApp.

Paste the link of your live invitation (the one from Settings, then Pages).

This page is open on your own laptop. Paste your live GitHub link above, otherwise the links will only work on your laptop.

Add a phone number after a | to send straight to that person. Without a number, WhatsApp lets you pick the chat. A 10-digit number is treated as an Indian number.

{name} becomes the guest's name and {link} becomes their personal link.
